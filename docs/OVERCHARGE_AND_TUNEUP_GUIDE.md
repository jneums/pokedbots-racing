# Overcharge & Perfect Tune-Up Guide

A simple guide to getting the most speed out of your PokedBot!

---

## ⚡ What is Overcharge?

**Overcharge** is a temporary speed boost you get when recharging your bot's battery.

### How it Works

When you recharge your bot, if the battery was low, you get a bonus:
- **The lower your battery before recharging = the bigger the boost!**
- Recharging at 10% battery gives you a much bigger boost than recharging at 80%

### What You Get

| Overcharge Amount | Speed/Accel Boost | Trade-off |
|-------------------|-------------------|-----------|
| 10% overcharge | +2.5% Speed & Acceleration | -1.3% Power Core & Stability |
| 20% overcharge | +5% Speed & Acceleration | -2.7% Power Core & Stability |
| 40% overcharge (max) | +10% Speed & Acceleration | -5.3% Power Core & Stability |

### The Trade-off

Overcharge makes you **faster** but slightly **less stable**. It's a risk vs reward choice:
- ✅ Great for short races where raw speed wins
- ⚠️ Risky for long races where stability prevents crashes

---

## 🔧 What is Perfect Tune-Up?

**Perfect Tune-Up** is a special bonus that lets you keep the speed boost from overcharge **without the downsides!**

### How to Get It

1. First, recharge your bot at low battery to get an overcharge
2. Then, repair your bot while you still have overcharge active
3. If you repair at the right time, you achieve Perfect Tune-Up!

### What Makes It "Perfect"

- Normal Overcharge: +Speed/Accel BUT -Power Core/Stability
- **Perfect Tune-Up: +Speed/Accel with NO PENALTY!** 🎉

This is the best of both worlds - all the speed, none of the instability.

## 🔮 The Resonance System

Each bot has a hidden "sweet spot" for when overcharge and tune-ups work best. This is called **Resonance**.

### The Mystery of Resonance

Your bot's optimal maintenance points are **not shown directly** - you need to discover them through experimentation and observation!

**What we know:**
- Optimal points fall somewhere between **20% and 80%** (battery or condition)
- Each bot has TWO sweet spots: one for recharging, one for repairing
- Sweet spots slowly drift over time (~weekly cycle with daily micro-shifts)
- Every bot's pattern is unique based on their token index

### The Resonance Zones

When you hit your bot's sweet spot:
- **Peak Zone (±3% of optimal)**: Maximum bonus - full overcharge or perfect tune-up!
- **Good Zone (±10% of optimal)**: Partial bonus (~60-70% of max effect)
- **Outside Zones**: Standard behavior - reduced bonus potential

### How to Find Your Sweet Spot

**Method 1: Experimentation**
1. Try recharging at different battery levels (30%, 40%, 50%, etc.)
2. Note how much overcharge you receive each time
3. Higher overcharge = closer to your resonance point!
4. Track results over several days to find the pattern

**Method 2: Watch the Results**
After each recharge/repair, pay attention to:
- Did you get more or less overcharge than expected?
- Did Perfect Tune-Up trigger when you thought it would?
- Compare results at different battery/condition levels

**Method 3: Keep a Log**
Track your maintenance with a simple spreadsheet:
| Date | Bot # | Battery Before | Overcharge Gained | Notes |
|------|-------|----------------|-------------------|-------|
| 1/15 | #4829 | 25% | 32% | Great result! |
| 1/16 | #4829 | 45% | 18% | Lower than expected |

Over time, you'll see patterns emerge!

### Tracking the Drift

Resonance points shift gradually. Tips for staying on top of it:
- **Weekly check-ins**: Test your sweet spots every few days
- **Notice changes**: If results suddenly drop, your window may have shifted
- **Don't assume**: Yesterday's perfect point might be off today

### Range Hints

Based on the system design:
- Optimal points are NEVER below 20% or above 80%
- The "center" tends to be around 50% but varies per bot
- Points can shift 10-20% over a week
- Morning vs evening can have slight variations (~daily micro-drift)

## 💡 Quick Strategy Tips

### For Casual Players
1. Recharge when battery is low (below 30%) for some overcharge
2. Don't stress about perfect timing - any overcharge helps!
3. Try a few different levels and stick with what works

### For Competitive Players
1. Experiment to find your bot's resonance window
2. Keep notes on what battery/condition levels gave best results
3. Re-test periodically as windows drift over time
4. If you got good overcharge, repair while it's active for Perfect Tune-Up
5. Time maintenance around your race schedule

```

        → Note which gave highest overcharge

                                    ↓

                                    ↓

```

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Does overcharge last forever?**
A: No! It's consumed after one race. Use it wisely!

**Q: How do I know if I hit the resonance sweet spot?**
A: You'll get noticeably higher overcharge! If recharging at 40% battery gives you 28% overcharge but 50% battery gives you 35%, then 50% is closer to your sweet spot.

**Q: What if I can't find my resonance window?**
A: Don't worry! Even without hitting peak resonance, you still get decent overcharge from low battery. The resonance system is a bonus for dedicated players, not a requirement.

**Q: Do all my bots have the same sweet spot?**
A: No! Each bot has unique resonance patterns based on their token index. You'll need to experiment with each one separately.

**Q: How often do the sweet spots change?**
A: They drift slowly over about a week, with small daily variations. Check your best spots every few days to stay current.

**Q: Is Perfect Tune-Up worth the effort?**
A: Absolutely! Getting +8% Speed/Accel with zero downsides is a huge advantage for competitive racing.

**Q: What's the possible range for sweet spots?**
A: Always between 20% and 80%. Your optimal recharge point won't be at 10% or 90% - it's somewhere in that middle range.
