# Racing Brackets (Classes) - Complete Guide

## HOW BRACKETS WORK

Your bot's **Rating** (average of max stats) determines which class of races you can enter. Rating is calculated from your bot's base stats + upgrade bonuses. This is separate from ELO, which is used for matchmaking within your class.

## 5 RACING CLASSES

### SCRAP CLASS (Beginner)

- **Rating Range:** 0-19
- **Entry Fee Multiplier:** 1.0× (e.g., 0.1 ICP Daily Sprint)
- **Platform Bonus:** 1.10 ICP (Daily Sprint), 0.80 ICP (Weekly League)
- **Competition:** Weakest bots, easiest wins

### JUNKER CLASS (Beginner)

- **Rating Range:** 20-29
- **Entry Fee Multiplier:** 1.5× (e.g., 0.15 ICP Daily Sprint)
- **Platform Bonus:** 1.60 ICP (Daily Sprint), 1.60 ICP (Weekly League)
- **Competition:** Low-tier bots

### RAIDER CLASS (Intermediate)

- **Rating Range:** 30-39
- **Entry Fee Multiplier:** 2.0× (e.g., 0.20 ICP Daily Sprint)
- **Platform Bonus:** 2.10 ICP (Daily Sprint), 2.40 ICP (Weekly League)
- **Competition:** Mid-tier bots

### ELITE CLASS (Advanced)

- **Rating Range:** 40-49
- **Entry Fee Multiplier:** 2.5× (e.g., 0.25 ICP Daily Sprint)
- **Platform Bonus:** 2.70 ICP (Daily Sprint), 3.20 ICP (Weekly League), 8.00 ICP (Monthly Cup)
- **Competition:** Strong bots, tougher field

### SILENTKLAN CLASS (Elite/Pro)

- **Rating Range:** 50+
- **Entry Fee Multiplier:** 3.0× (e.g., 0.30 ICP Daily Sprint)
- **Platform Bonus:** 3.20 ICP (Daily Sprint), 4.80 ICP (Weekly League), 12.00 ICP (Monthly Cup)
- **Competition:** Best bots only

## KEY POINTS

- You can ONLY enter races in your current Rating class
- **Entry fees use a multiplier system:** Each event type has a base entry fee, then your class multiplier is applied (1.0× Scrap through 3.0× SilentKlan)
- Winning = +ELO (for matchmaking within class), Losing = -ELO
- Rating determines class eligibility, ELO determines opponents within that class
- Prize pools scale with class difficulty and include platform bonuses
- All racers earn parts based on their class (Scrap=18, Junker=25, Raider=34, Elite=43, SilentKlan=50 base parts) with position multipliers (1st=1.5×, 2nd=1.25×, 3rd=1.1×)
- Upgrade your stats to increase Rating and access higher classes

## ⚠️ BRACKET JUMPING WARNING

**Rating determines your race class!** Getting lucky with upgrades can accidentally bounce you into a higher bracket:

- **The Problem:** You're at Rating 19 (Scrap class) and upgrade a stat. You get a double point upgrade (+2 instead of +1) and your rating jumps to 20 → **instantly moved to Junker class** with higher entry fees and tougher competition!

- **Safe Approach:** If you're near a bracket boundary (e.g., 19.5 rating), avoid upgrading stats that would push you over. Wait until you have 2+ stats ready to upgrade at once, or focus on different stats to stay balanced.

- **Bracket Boundaries to Watch:**
  - Rating 19 → 20 (Scrap to Junker)
  - Rating 29 → 30 (Junker to Raider)
  - Rating 39 → 40 (Raider to Elite)
  - Rating 49 → 50 (Elite to SilentKlan)

- **Recommendation:** When within 2 points of a bracket jump, stop upgrading that stat until you're ready for the higher class. The double point lottery can catch you off guard!
