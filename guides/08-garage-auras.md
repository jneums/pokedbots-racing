# Garage Auras - Collection Bonuses

## WHAT ARE GARAGE AURAS?

Garage auras are **collection bonuses** that apply to ALL bots in your garage when you own multiple bots of the same faction. The more bots of a faction you collect, the stronger the bonuses become.

**Key Features:**
- Bonuses apply to **ALL** your bots, not just the faction that grants them
- **Currently active bonuses:** Cost reductions, yield bonuses, and drain reductions
- ⚠️ **Stat-based auras (e.g., +Speed, +Stability) are currently disabled** while the system is being reworked
- Cost bonuses reduce ICP expenses (repairs, recharges, upgrades)
- Yield bonuses increase earnings (race prizes, scavenging parts)
- Drain bonuses reduce scavenging battery/condition costs

## ⚠️ STAT AURAS (CURRENTLY DISABLED)

The stat-based aura system (e.g., UltimateMaster giving +5 all stats, Bee giving +5 Speed, etc.) is **currently disabled** while being reworked. The system returns `{0, 0, 0, 0}` for all factions.

The sections below describe stat auras as they were **originally designed**. They may return in a future update with rebalanced values. For now, only **cost/yield/drain multiplier auras** are active (see Active Bonuses section below).

## STRATEGY TIPS

### For New Players
- **Food** (2 bots) reduces recharge cooldown by 10% — race more often
- **Industrial** (2 bots) reduces repair costs by 10% — cheaper maintenance
- **Game** (2 bots) reduces upgrade costs by 5% — cheaper progression
- Don't chase specific factions early — focus on building a functional garage

### For Scavengers
- **Dead** (4-6 bots) gives +20-30% scavenging parts — massive parts advantage
- **Ultimate** (2-3 bots) gives -15-30% scavenging drain — longer missions
- These two combine well: more parts AND less drain

### For Competitive Racers
- **Golden** (3 bots) gives +15% race prize earnings — compounds over time
- While stat auras are disabled, focus on cost/yield bonuses
- Reduced maintenance costs mean higher net profit from racing

### Combining Bonuses
Cost/yield multiplier auras **stack multiplicatively** with each other:
- Dead (parts) + Ultimate (drain) = more parts per hour AND longer missions
- Game (upgrade cost) + Industrial (repair cost) + Food (cooldown) = cheaper everything
- Golden (prize bonus) + any cost reduction = maximum profit

## ECONOMIC IMPACT

### Cost Bonuses (Save ICP)
- **Game** (6 bots): Save 15% on all upgrades
- **Industrial** (6 bots): Save 30% on repairs
- **Food** (6 bots): Recharge cooldown reduced by 30%

### Yield Bonuses (Earn More)
- **Golden** (3 bots): +15% race earnings compounds rapidly
- **Dead** (6 bots): +30% parts generation = faster self-upgrading
- **Ultimate** (3 bots): -30% scavenging drain = longer missions, more parts

## HOW TO BUILD YOUR COLLECTION

1. **Choose your focus:** Cost reduction (Game/Industrial/Food) vs Yield bonuses (Golden/Dead/Ultimate)
2. **Start with 2-3 bots** of one faction to activate first tier bonuses
3. **Scale gradually** - most factions max out at 3-6 bots
4. **Mix strategically** - combine yield and cost factions for balanced benefits
5. **Consider resale** - collection bonuses make your entire garage more valuable
6. **Watch for stat aura re-enablement** - when stat auras return, faction composition will matter even more

## IMPORTANT NOTES

- Bonuses apply immediately when you acquire the required bot count
- Bonuses disappear if you sell/transfer bots and drop below the threshold
- All bots in your garage benefit, regardless of their own faction
- Marketplace bots do NOT count toward your bonuses (must be in your garage)
- Bonuses persist across races - they're always active
