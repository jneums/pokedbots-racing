## 🏆 REGULAR EVENTS

### Weekly League Championship
**When:** Every Sunday at 8:00 PM UTC  
**Entry Fee:** 0.8 ICP (varies by division)  
**Divisions:** All (Scrap, Junker, Raider, Elite, Silent Klan)  
**Prize Pool Bonus:** +2 ICP platform contribution  
**Duration:** ~2 hours

The premier weekly event for all racers! Register anytime between Friday evening and 30 minutes before start time. Once registration closes, we'll split racers into balanced heats based on who signed up. Winners get double championship points!

**Perfect for:** Weekly competitive racing, all skill levels

**Registration:**
- Opens: Friday 8:00 PM UTC (48 hours before)
- Closes: Sunday 7:30 PM UTC (30 min before)
- Cancellation: Free until Friday, decreasing refunds after

## 🎫 REGISTRATION TIPS

### Payment & Cancellation
- Pay entry fee when you register (held in escrow)
- Cancel early = full refund
- Cancel late = reduced refund (50% → 25% → 0%)
- Don't cancel after registration closes!

### Blind Registration
- You won't see who else registered until registration closes
- Prevents "dodging" top competitors
- Creates more authentic matchmaking
- Everyone finds out opponents together

### Heat Assignment
Events use different strategies:
- **Balanced (Snake):** Mixed skill levels for fair competition
- **Skill-Tiered:** Top performers race together, beginners protected
- **Top/Bottom Split:** Extremes separated
- **Random:** Pure chaos, anyone against anyone

### Managing Multiple Bots
- Each bot registers separately
- Can enter multiple bots in same event (different heats)
- Plan your lineup strategically!

## ❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: What happens if not enough racers sign up?**  
A: Event needs minimum registrations (usually 4-8). If we don't hit minimum, event is cancelled and everyone gets full refund.

**Q: Can I register for multiple events at once?**  
A: Yes! Plan your weekend. Just make sure your bot can handle the schedule.

**Q: What if my bot's condition is low?**  
A: You can still register, but you'll need to repair/recharge before the races start. Plan ahead!

**Q: How are heats decided?**  
A: Depends on the event! Some balance by skill, some group top players, some are random. Check event details.

**Q: What are "multi-stage" events?**  
A: Events where you race multiple times. Register once, compete in all scheduled races automatically.

**Q: Can I see my opponents before racing?**  
A: Not until registration closes! This prevents dodging and creates fair matchups.

**Q: What's the deal with platform bonuses?**  
A: We add ICP to prize pools to subsidize racing and attract more competitors. Bigger events = bigger bonuses!

**Q: Do I get points for participation?**  
A: Yes! Championship points for top finishers, with multipliers for bigger events. Weekly League = 2x, Champions Cup = 5x!
