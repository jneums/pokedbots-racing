# Bot Dedication System - Complete Guide

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** Dedication points are **only earned through the website UI**. Actions performed via MCP tools (Claude, ChatGPT, or other AI assistants) **do not count** toward dedication. You must use the PokedBots website to earn DP from racing, scavenging, and maintenance activities.

## WHAT IS DEDICATION?

The Dedication System rewards you for the time and effort you put into your bots. Whether you're racing competitively, scavenging for parts, or investing ICP in upgrades - every action builds your bot's dedication and unlocks permanent bonuses.

Think of it as a **loyalty program** - your most active bots earn special perks that make them even better at racing and scavenging.

## HOW IT WORKS

> **Note:** All DP must be earned through the website interface. MCP tool actions do not generate dedication points.

Your bots earn **Dedication Points (DP)** from both **activities** and **investments**:

### Activity DP (Free to Earn!)
| Activity | DP Earned |
|----------|-----------|
| Completing a race | 5 DP |
| Winning a race (1st place) | +15 DP bonus |
| Podium finish (2nd-3rd) | +8 DP bonus |
| Completing a scavenging mission | 3 DP |
| Collecting parts (per 10 parts) | 1 DP |
| Restoring battery (per 25 battery at Charging Station) | 1 DP |
| Restoring condition (per 10 condition at Repair Bay) | 1 DP |

### Investment DP (When You Spend ICP)
| Activity | DP Earned |
|----------|-----------|
| Any ICP spent | 100 DP per ICP |

This includes upgrades, repairs, recharges, race entries, and event fees.

**The key insight:** A dedicated free-to-play racer can reach the same tiers as someone who spends ICP - it just takes more races!

## DEDICATION TIERS

As your bot accumulates DP, it climbs through tiers that unlock increasingly powerful bonuses:

### ⭐ Tier 1: Dedicated (700 DP)
*~140 races, or ~7 ICP invested, or a mix of both*
- +1 Speed
- +1% terrain bonus
- 1.10x scavenging yield
- 10% faster recharge cooldown

### 🌟 Tier 2: Veteran (2,700 DP)
*~540 races, or ~27 ICP invested, or a mix of both*
- +1 Speed, +1 Acceleration
- +2% terrain bonus
- 1.15x scavenging yield
- 20% faster recharge cooldown
- 10% faster repair cooldown

### 💫 Tier 3: Champion (8,000 DP)
*~1,600 races, or ~80 ICP invested, or a mix of both*
- +2 Speed, +1 Acceleration, +1 Power Core
- +3% terrain bonus
- 1.20x scavenging yield
- 30% faster recharge cooldown
- 20% faster repair cooldown
- 5% upgrade cost discount

### 🏆 Tier 4: Elite (20,000 DP)
*~4,000 races, or ~200 ICP invested, or a mix of both*
- +2 Speed, +2 Acceleration, +1 Power Core, +1 Stability
- +4% terrain bonus
- 1.25x scavenging yield
- 40% faster recharge cooldown
- 30% faster repair cooldown
- 10% upgrade cost discount

### 👑 Tier 5: Legend (60,000 DP)
*~12,000 races, or ~600 ICP invested, or a mix of both*
- +3 to ALL stats (Speed, Acceleration, Power Core, Stability)
- +5% terrain bonus
- 1.30x scavenging yield
- 50% faster recharge cooldown
- 40% faster repair cooldown
- 15% upgrade cost discount

## STRATEGY TIPS

### For Free-to-Play Players
- **Race consistently** with the same bots - every race earns 5 DP (plus bonuses for winning!)
- **Scavenging counts too!** Each completed mission earns 3 DP, plus bonus DP for parts collected
- **Use Repair Bay & Charging Station** - these free maintenance zones keep your bots racing AND earn DP for battery/condition restored
- Focus on 2-3 main bots to climb tiers faster rather than spreading across many
- Tier 1 (Dedicated) takes ~140 races - achievable in a couple months of regular play

### For Active Racers
- Your daily racing naturally builds dedication over time
- Enter as many races as you can - you earn 5 DP per race, plus 15 DP bonus for wins and 8 DP for podiums
- Mix racing with scavenging to maximize DP while your battery/condition recharges
- Winning matters! A bot that wins regularly earns DP much faster than one that just participates

### For Scavenger Fleets
- Higher dedication = more parts per mission (up to 30% bonus at Legend)
- Each scavenging mission earns 3 DP, plus 1 DP per 10 parts collected
- The reduced cooldowns at higher tiers mean more maintenance efficiency
- Consider dedicating specific bots to scavenging to build their tiers

### For Spenders
- ICP spending accelerates dedication significantly (100 DP per ICP)
- All ICP activities contribute: upgrades, repairs, recharges, race entries
- Best value: spend on bots you're already actively using to combine both DP sources

### Reaching Each Tier
- **Tier 1 (Dedicated, 700 DP):** ~140 races OR ~7 ICP - first milestone
- **Tier 2 (Veteran, 2,700 DP):** ~540 races OR ~27 ICP - mid-term goal
- **Tier 3 (Champion, 8,000 DP):** ~1,600 races OR ~80 ICP - serious dedication
- **Tier 4 (Elite, 20,000 DP):** Long-term commitment through consistent play
- **Tier 5 (Legend, 60,000 DP):** Reserved for your most-played bots over years

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Q: Can I reach high tiers without spending ICP?**
A: Yes, but it takes significant time. Every race earns 5 DP (plus bonuses), and scavenging earns 3 DP per mission. Free maintenance at Repair Bay and Charging Station also earns small DP amounts. A dedicated F2P player can reach Veteran tier through pure activity, though higher tiers are designed to require serious long-term commitment.

**Q: Does dedication transfer if I sell my bot?**
A: Yes! Dedication stays with the bot, not the owner. A Legend-tier bot keeps all its bonuses when sold - which could make it more valuable on the marketplace!

**Q: Can I lose dedication?**
A: No, dedication only goes up. Respeccing your bot doesn't affect dedication.

**Q: Do the stat bonuses count toward my bot's rating/class?**
A: No, dedication bonuses are applied during races but don't change your visible stats or race class eligibility. This means a lower-class bot can punch above its weight with high dedication!

**Q: What's the fastest way to earn DP as F2P?**
A: Win races! You get 5 DP base + 15 DP win bonus = 20 DP per win. Even podium finishes give +8 DP bonus. Between races, scavenge and use Repair Bay/Charging Station for free maintenance that also earns small DP.

**Q: Is it better to spread activity across many bots or focus on a few?**
A: Focus on a few! The benefits scale with tier, so one Veteran bot is much stronger than five Rookies with scattered DP.

**Q: Do I earn DP from Repair Bay and Charging Station?**
A: Yes! You earn 3 DP for completing the mission, plus 1 DP per 25 battery restored (Charging Station) or 1 DP per 10 condition restored (Repair Bay). Free DP while maintaining your bots!

**Q: Why do higher tiers focus on Speed bonuses?**
A: The system rewards dedication with Speed first (the most impactful racing stat), then adds Acceleration, Power Core, and finally Stability at the highest tiers. Only Legend tier gets the full +3 to all stats.

## SUMMARY

The Dedication System rewards **how much you play**, not just how much you spend. Whether you're racing daily, scavenging for parts, or investing ICP in your favorites - every action builds dedication and makes your bots stronger.

Focus on your favorite bots, stay active, and watch them climb the tiers over time. The bonuses are permanent and stack with everything else - giving dedicated players a meaningful edge in both racing and scavenging.
